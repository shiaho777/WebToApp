隐私说明

WebToApp 把你提交的网址或 HTML 做成可安装的应用，并提供下载页和应用市场。

设备标识

网站用一个随机设备标识记住你在这台设备上生成过的应用。它存在名为 webtoapp_device_fingerprint 的 Cookie 里，也存在本机存储中，大约保留一年。没有账号，这个标识也不含你的姓名。

广告

首页、应用市场和下载页使用 Google AdSense 展示广告。Google 及合作方会使用 Cookie，根据你之前访问过的网站投放广告。你可以在 Google 广告设置 里关闭个性化广告。Google 对广告 Cookie 的说明见 How Google uses cookies in advertising。

广告只出现在 WebToApp 自己的页面上，不会放进你打包的网站或生成的应用里。

Privacy

WebToApp turns a URL or an HTML file you submit into an installable app, with a download page and a public market.

Device id

The site keeps a random device id so it can list the apps generated on this device. The id is stored in a cookie named webtoapp_device_fingerprint and in local storage, for about a year. There is no account, and the id is not your name.

Advertising

The home page, the market, and download pages show ads through Google AdSense. Google and its partners use cookies to serve ads based on visits to this and other sites. You can turn off personalized ads at Google Ads Settings. Google describes these cookies at How Google uses cookies in advertising.

Ads stay on WebToApp’s own pages. They are not placed inside the site you package or inside the generated app.
